Abstract— I am a ML researcher at Sony Research India, working on personalization and optimization. Previously, I worked on domain adaptation with the STARS Research Team at Inria Centre at Université Côte d’Azur. I also interned at the University of Alberta, where I worked on adversarial robustness, and spent a “bit” of time at Microsoft as an SDE intern, developing testbeds for 5G infrastructure. As an undergraduate I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at the Delhi Technological University, India.
Index Terms— recommender systems, personalization, optimization, domain adaptation, BERT, adversarial robustness.
