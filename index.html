Machine Learning Researcher

Tushar Prakash

Personalization, optimization & recommender systems at Sony Research India

I am a ML researcher at Sony Research India, working on personalization and optimization. Previously I worked on domain adaptation with the STARS Research Team at Inria, Université Côte d’Azur, interned at the University of Alberta on adversarial robustness, and spent a “bit” of time at Microsoft as an SDE intern building testbeds for 5G infrastructure. I studied Electronics & Communication Engineering at Delhi Technological University. Feel free to reach out by email!
